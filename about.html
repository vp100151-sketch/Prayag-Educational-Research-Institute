About Us

हमारे बारे में About the Institute

Prayag Educational Research Institute Hindi medium ke CA Foundation, Intermediate (IPCC) aur Final students ke liye seedha, bharosemand aur muft study material banata hai — taaki bhasha kabhi bhi kisi ke sapno ke beech me deewaar na bane.

हमारा उद्देश्य

Bahut se CA Foundation, Intermediate (IPCC) aur Final students Hindi medium se aate hain, lekin zyadatar study material sirf English me milta hai. Isi gap ko bharne ke liye Prayag Educational Research Institute ne yeh chapter-wise, Hindi bhasha me likha gaya material taiyar kiya hai — bilkul muft, sabke liye.

हम क्या मानते हैं

  • 01 Gyan bhasha ka mohtaj nahi hona chahiye — jo bhi samajh aaye, usi bhasha me seekhna sabse behtar hai.
  • 02 Achha study material har student tak muft me pahunchna chahiye.
  • 03 Chapter-wise, simple aur exam-focused content hi sabse zyada kaam aata hai.
